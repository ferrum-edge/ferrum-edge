Isolated reproduction of `ferrum-edge reload --pid 0` on
f4b62fe7d4620d81c836d6418f2957269ac6e115 (linux x86_64).

Command:
  setsid /workspace/target/debug/ferrum-edge reload --pid 0

Observed:
  bash: Hangup
  process exit 129 (128 + SIGHUP)

Root cause:
  src/cli.rs execute_reload runs `kill -HUP <pid>` with no lower bound.
  POSIX/Linux `kill -HUP 0` means "signal my process group", not "PID 0".
  clap accepts u32 so 0 is a legal --pid.

Expected:
  reject pid 0 (and other non-positive / special PIDs) with a diagnostic.
