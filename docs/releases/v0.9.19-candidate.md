This pre-publication record covers the protected merge for the 0.9.19
replacement release. The immutable v0.9.18 tag was created but never
published; see the [ARM64 recovery record](v0.9.18-arm64-recovery.md).

[changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md#0919---2026-10-27) and
[upgrade guide](../upgrade_guide.md#upgrading-to-0919). The latest published

## Admitted recovery source

things:

- provisioned and verified 32 GiB of swap in the protected ARM64 Cross
  producer;
- raised that job's timeout to 240 minutes and refroze its contract;
- prepared 0.9.19.

GPT-6.1 Sol and Muse (xhigh) reported CLEAN on its final head
`d7dd7c114aaa386862d81c23eaaf004096bf5cd8`.
[Qualification run 40430736565](https://github.com/ferrum-edge/ferrum-edge/actions/runs/40430736565)
ran a non-publishing copy of that producer on a hosted `ubuntu-24.04` runner.
The swap step activated, and the fat-LTO compile finished in 40 minutes
52 seconds.

PR #6957 changes protected producer and verifier surfaces. Its own head's CI
policy and Trusted Cross Build Policy checks therefore failed by design.
`Tests` failed only because CI planning stopped. It was merged through an
administrator ruleset bypass as a squash commit at
`8a35ddf8f84b2e0c4b1ed74f3a56c3e4f1fee27c`. That commit cannot itself be a
release commit.

## Protected merge

This record is the protected follow-up. Its PR is cut from main at
`8a35ddf8f84b2e0c4b1ed74f3a56c3e4f1fee27c` and merged with a **merge
commit**. Its reviewed head is the second parent, its trusted policy and full
CI run against the admitted producer, and the merge tree equals that head's
tree. **Start Production Release** then runs for `v0.9.19` from that merge
commit. A post-publication record replaces this candidate record once the
release, its images, and the matching `contracts-edge-0.9.19` tag are
verified.
